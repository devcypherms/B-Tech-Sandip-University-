The Bihar Student Credit Card

Sandip University accepts the Bihar Student Credit Card, the state government's education loan for students who have passed 10+2 and hold Bihar domicile. It is the route most families here use, and it is why the phrase engineering college with student credit card is searched at all.

The loan is applied for through the state, not through the university. You register online on the Bihar government portal and then attend your DRCC in person within 60 days to complete verification. Our admission cell prepares the file with your family so nobody works it out alone.

Approval and the sanctioned amount rest with the Government of Bihar, so the university works to the state's process rather than around it.

The portal is the Bihar Student Credit Card scheme under 7 Nishchay — Yuva Up Mission. Ask on the form below and the admission cell will send you the current link, the scheme document and your nearest DRCC address together.

Maximum loan
₹4,00,000
Interest
Nil. The revised state scheme is interest-free for every applicant.
Repayment
Repayment starts a year after the course ends: up to 84 monthly instalments, and up to 120 on a loan above ₹2,00,000.
Security
No property or deposit is pledged. A parent, spouse or guardian signs as co-applicant.
Age limit
25 years on the date of application.
Documents
Aadhaar for you and your co-applicant, your marksheets and certificates, a bank passbook showing IFSC, the admission letter and fee statement from the university, address proof and two photographs each. Nothing is uploaded online — you carry the originals to the DRCC.

Admission process 2026

Four steps. No entrance exam.

JEE Main scores are considered where you have them, and direct admission on your 10+2 marks is available where you do not.

  1. Apply online, or call

    Fill the form on this page or call the admission cell. You need your 10+2 marksheet — or your predicted marks if the result is still awaited.

  2. Documents and eligibility

    Marksheets, transfer and character certificates, Aadhaar and photographs are verified against the branch you have asked for. Within 7 days of applying.

  3. Counselling and branch allotment

    The admission cell confirms your branch, your scholarship band and your first-year figure in writing. Rolling, from June 2026. Branch allotment goes by merit and by order of application.

  4. Fee payment and enrolment

    Pay directly, or begin the Student Credit Card file with the admission cell. Within 10 days of branch allotment, and the session begins 15 October 2026.
